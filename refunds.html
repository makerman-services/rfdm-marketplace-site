Refunds and your rights

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Australia only

When checkout opens, it will be available to buyers in Australia only. If your card or billing address is outside Australia, we cancel the payment hold and you are not charged. Your bank may show a pending amount for a few days until it drops off.

14-day refund

Changed your mind? You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, for any reason. Email makermanservices@gmail.com with your order email. This is on top of your rights below.

AI usage charges

AI usage charges are between you and your AI provider and aren't part of our refund. Your normal rights under Australian Consumer Law still apply.

Your rights

Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled: to cancel your service contract with us; and to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced value. You are also entitled to choose a refund or replacement for major failures with goods. If a failure with the goods or a service does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have the failure rectified in a reasonable time. If this is not done you are entitled to a refund for the goods and to cancel the contract for the service and obtain a refund of any unused portion. You are also entitled to be compensated for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage from a failure in the goods or service.

What counts as a failure

For digital products, a failure could include a copy or setup guide that is missing, won't open, or doesn't match its listing. Email us and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you in line with your rights above.

How to ask

Email makermanservices@gmail.com with your order email and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
