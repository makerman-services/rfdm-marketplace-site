Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

This notice covers RFDM Marketplace, run by Makerman Services, ABN 22 791 780 670. Contact us about privacy at makermanservices@gmail.com. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you buy, Stripe (our payment provider) collects your name, email, card details and billing address. We receive your email, the bot you bought, the amount, and your card and billing country (we sell to buyers in Australia only). We never see your full card number. We don't use accounts or sign-in. When you start a checkout, Stripe creates a customer record with your country. If you don't finish paying, it holds nothing else.

Why

To deliver your download, check your purchase, give refunds, answer your emails and keep records the law requires. We use your card's issuing country and billing country to check the purchase is from Australia before we take payment.

Who helps us

Stripe (payments, may store data overseas, including in the USA), GitHub, Inc. (USA, hosts this site on GitHub Pages and may log visitor IP addresses and similar technical data for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement) and Gmail (support email). This site itself uses no cookies or trackers. When you use your bot in Google AI Studio, that's between you and Google under Google's terms; we don't see your chats.

Marketing

We don't send marketing email unless you opt in separately.

Access and correction / complaints

Email makermanservices@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can contact the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).

Keeping and deleting

We don't run accounts, so there's nothing to close. We keep order records (your email, what you bought, the amount, the date and your card and billing country) for 5 years, because Australian tax law requires business records. Stripe keeps payment records under its own privacy policy. Your download link keeps working so you can download again, and we can turn it off if you ask. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of anything we don't need to keep, email makermanservices@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Waitlist

The waitlist is not open yet, and this site does not collect waitlist details yet. If you join the RFDM Marketplace waitlist, Makerman Services collects your email address (and your first name if you give it) to tell you when RFDM Marketplace opens and send RFDM Marketplace news. We use MailerLite to store your details and send these emails, and MailerLite may store them on servers outside Australia. We don't sell your details or share them for anyone else's marketing. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we act on it within 5 business days. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your details at makermanservices@gmail.com. If you don't give your email, you just won't get the update.

Emails

We will only send marketing emails if you tick the consent box, and every email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you. If you're unhappy with how we handled a privacy matter, email us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
