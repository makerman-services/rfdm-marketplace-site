Content Manager

Coming soon

A blank copy of the crew member who drafts our posts. The original stays on board with us. Your copy writes posts, captions and short scripts in your brand voice, then hands them to you to check. It never publishes anything itself.

What it does

  • Plans a simple weekly list of post ideas from the topics you give it.
  • Drafts captions and short video scripts in the tone you set.
  • Suggests alt text for each image you plan to use.
  • When you ask, lists what it has drafted in this chat.
